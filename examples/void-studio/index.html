( Questions we get )
How long does an identity take?+

Eight to twelve weeks for most identities. Adding a website pushes it to four or five months. The biggest variable is how quickly decisions get made on your side.

Do you work with small companies?+

Yes. About half our clients are teams of fewer than ten. Small companies tend to make braver choices because fewer people have to agree.

How do you price a project?+

A fixed fee, agreed after a first call and a written scope. You know the full cost before we start, and it only changes if the scope does.

Studio or agency?+

A studio. We define what the brand is before deciding how it should advertise. An agency usually starts from the campaign and works backwards.
