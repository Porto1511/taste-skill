Our approach

Every plot is a one-off.

No two gardens share the same light, soil, slope or people. So we don't start from a catalogue. We start by walking the space with you, noticing where the sun lands in the evening, where the ground stays wet and which views are worth framing.

From there, design, hard landscaping and planting are handled as one joined-up piece of work, so the finished garden reads as a single idea rather than a collection of jobs.

Services

From first sketch to first bloom.

Take on one piece or the whole garden. Every job is quoted individually after a site visit.

  1. 01

    Garden design

    Layouts, planting plans and visuals built around your plot, light and the way you want to use the space.

  2. 02

    Hard landscaping

    Patios, paving, paths, steps, walls and edging, set out to last and laid to suit the rest of the garden.

  3. 03

    Planting schemes

    Borders, hedging, trees and grasses chosen for your soil and aspect, with interest through every season.

  4. 04

    Lawns & turfing

    Ground preparation, levelling and new turf, or bringing a tired lawn back into good shape.

  5. 05

    Fencing, decking & structures

    Boundaries, screens, decks and garden structures that give the space shape, shelter and privacy.

  6. 06

    Seasonal maintenance

    Pruning, hedge cutting, mowing and borders kept tidy, so the garden keeps looking the way it was designed to.
